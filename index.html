MIX YouTube

Your video. Your final say.

A private, local workflow for preparing YouTube videos, reviewing English publishing copy with Chinese translations, and publishing through the official YouTube API.

MIX 是自用的视频制作与发布工具。英文文案附中文对照，审完片并核对文案后，一次明确确认即可上传并公开。

From draft to a deliberate release

  1. Prepare and review. Draft English titles, descriptions and relevant hashtags from the video's supplied story and subtitles. Internal project names stay separate from publishing titles.
  2. Confirm upload and public release. The owner reviews the final file, channel, audience and bilingual publishing copy, then explicitly approves uploading this video directly as public.
  3. Follow the result. MIX saves the returned video ID and checks the actual metadata and visibility. YouTube processing and platform restrictions can still affect availability.

No video is uploaded or made public simply because the Google account is connected.

How Google access is used

MIX YouTube uses YouTube API Services to verify the connected channel, upload an owner-approved video with its title, description and tags, read back processing and publishing details, and change visibility after approval.

Google handles sign-in and consent. MIX does not ask for or store your Google password. A fixed channel ID identifies the target, so changing its display name does not change the destination.

MIX is operated by Zhang Longsheng (张隆生) for personal use and a limited, known team. This public website explains the tool; it does not provide public access to the local application or its connected channel.
